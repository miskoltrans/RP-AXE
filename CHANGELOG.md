# Changelog

The in-game copy (shown once per new version by KerbalChangelog) is
`GameData/RP-AXE/RP-AXE-Changelog.cfg`; keep the two in step.

## 0.9.1 — Sol support, Harmony fixes

- **Fixed: AXE's Harmony patches on current CKAN installs.** RealFuels 15.16 changed the engine-config
  window, the engine-config purchase gate failed to patch, and Harmony skipped every AXE patch after
  it. Patches now apply one by one, and the gate supports both RealFuels versions.
- **Fixed: every AXE Harmony patch was applied twice.** The avionics overload corruption ran twice per
  physics tick, so 0.9.0's penalty was roughly double the design: misdirection up to twice the angle,
  twice the noise, overgain squared. RCS translation corruption also leaked past the thrusters. Over
  capacity, a craft now flies at the designed strength, which is noticeably gentler. Also fixed:
  engine-upgrade tooltip stats shown twice, and the bimodal-toggle message posted twice.
- **Sol support:** `GameData/RP-AXE/design/Sol/` (generated by `tools/gen_sol_compat.py`) keeps a Sol
  career on the RSS balance: per-body science, Earth sub-40 km removal, gas-giant heat, SCANsat
  altitudes, ground-station antennas, outer-planet resources. New `RP-AXE-Express-Sol.ckan` preset;
  the RSS texture choice in `RP-AXE.netkan` accepts Sol-Core. Guide: `docs/SOL.md`.

## 0.9.0 — public beta (first release)

- **Tech tree:** a from-scratch, discipline-based tree of ~530 breakthrough nodes compiled from
  `tree/nodes.yaml`, science-gated, with an in-game R&D render (discipline colours, hover lineage,
  OR-gates drawn dashed).
- **Ported from RP-1:** the science instrument suite and crewed science, 35 programs and their
  contracts (running on AXE's own ContractConfigurator types), the engine-config upgrade ladders,
  the DSN tracking-station levels, airlaunch, range safety, non-reentry-rated parts, dev parts.
- **AXE's own:** avionics with steerable-mass capacity and control corruption on overload instead
  of a lock; an in-editor flight simulator; a funds economy with no build times, tooling,
  maintenance, crew training or reliability grind; free launch-site switching; a capstone +
  program-completion payout model.
- **KerbalismLab:** 65 crewed-station experiments integrated and rebalanced.
- **Packaging:** CKAN metadata (`RP-AXE.netkan`), KSP-AVC version file, `tools/package_release.py`.
